Privacy Policy — Crown Grid

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Crown Grid is an offline logic-puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: Crown Grid works fully offline, requires no account, and does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.

1. Data we collect

None on our servers. Crown Grid has no backend and no account system. Everything the game stores — your puzzle progress, solved levels, daily streak, statistics, and settings — is kept only on your device in the app's local storage.

2. Internet and network

The app does not request the Internet permission and makes no network connections. Background music is bundled inside the app; nothing is streamed or downloaded at runtime.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration — used only for haptic feedback when you tap the board.

Crown Grid contains no ads and no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

4. Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose your data to anyone. Your data never leaves your device, so there is no third party that receives it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed. You can reset progress inside the app, or remove all data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Crown Grid. Because we hold no copy, uninstalling permanently deletes everything.

6. Children's privacy

Crown Grid is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children. As the app collects no personal data at all, no such data is processed.

7. Music licensing

Background music tracks are used under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain) dedication and require no attribution.
